## About
The Founding Dev Design Hackathon is an online hackathon over one weekend. Build a stunning landing page that converts. Win a share of the $1,000 cash prize pool, plus AI credits to keep building on Founding.dev. You design and publish a landing page in Founding.dev on any topic, make it look great, and share it on LinkedIn. 

- **Format**: Online · Worldwide
- **Dates**: November 6 – November 9, 2026. Submissions close November 9, winners announced November 11.
- **Prizes**: $1,000 in cash pool + AI credits
- **Cost**: Free to participate. Credits are provided, so there's no cost to your members.
- **Discord**: Join the [Founding.dev Discord](https://discord.gg/47Wc2NfA6p) for community updates, questions, and winner announcements.

## How the design hackathon works

Three steps. One weekend.

- **Step 1 · Register**: Sign up below. Spots are limited, so we'll email you to confirm yours. Accepted builders get $10 of AI build credits when the hackathon starts, on top of the free trial.
- **Step 2 · Build**: Design a landing page on Founding.dev. Any topic. Make it look great. Solo or with one teammate.
- **Step 3 · Publish, post, submit**: Publish what you've created, post it on LinkedIn tagging `@Founding Dev` add your preview link. And [Submit you entry](https://founding.dev/hackathon/design/submission) here. You need at least **12 reposts** on your post to be judged.

## Rules

- Open to anyone at any skill level worldwide, including software engineers, frontend developers, and designers.
- Free to participate. Credits are provided, so there is no cost to members.
- Enter solo or as a team of 2. Add your teammate's email when you register.
- Design a landing page on **Founding.dev**. Any topic. Make it look great.
- No coding required. You design and publish the page in the **Founding.dev** landing page builder.
- Publish what you've created, post it on **LinkedIn** tagging `@Founding Dev`, then send us your links.
- You need at least **12 real reposts** on your **LinkedIn post** to be judged.
- Judges score each design on visual design, usability and clarity, creativity, and content and conversion. More reposts raise that score, up to 1.3 times.
- The Judges' Choice prize goes to the best design on its own, reposts aside.
- You keep the page you build. A few of the best designs will be added to the **Founding.dev** template library.

## FAQ

### What is the Founding.dev Design Hackathon?
An online design hackathon over one weekend. You build a landing page on Founding.dev, publish it, and share it on LinkedIn. Three winners split $1,000 in cash, and each gets 1 month of Unleash ($59).

### When is it?
You build from Friday, November 6, 12:00 AM PT to Monday, November 9, 11:59 PM PT. In the UK that's Friday 8:00 AM to Tuesday 7:59 AM. In India it's Friday 1:30 PM to Tuesday 1:29 PM. Winners are announced on Wednesday, November 11.

### How are winners picked?
You need at least 12 real reposts on your LinkedIn post to be judged. Judges score each design on visual design, usability and clarity, creativity, and content and conversion. More reposts raise that score, up to 1.3 times. The Judges' Choice prize goes to the best design on its own, reposts aside. The full rules have every detail.

### What happens to my design?
It's yours. You keep the page you build. We may share entries, and we'll add a few of the best to the Founding.dev template library.

### Does it cost anything?
No. Registration is free. Everyone who's accepted gets $10 of AI build credits on top of the normal free trial. The credits land when the hackathon starts and last 14 days. Existing Founding.dev users get them too. If you run out mid-build, you can pick a plan and top up in the app.

### Do I need to code?
No. You design and publish the page in the Founding.dev landing page builder.

### Can I enter as a team?
Yes. Enter solo or as a team of 2. Add your teammate's email when you register.

### Where to submit?
Create and publish your landing page using Founding.dev, share it on LinkedIn tagging `@Founding Dev`, and submit your published page and LinkedIn post links. A minimum of **12 genuine reposts** is required to qualify for judging.

* Submission Link: [https://founding.dev/hackathon/design/submission](https://founding.dev/hackathon/design/submission)


### Where do I ask questions during the weekend?
In the [Founding.dev Discord](https://discord.gg/47Wc2NfA6p). The team is there all weekend, and it's where the winners are announced too.
